Privacy Policy for Car Calculator

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Car Calculator is designed to help users estimate vehicle affordability, ownership costs, financing scenarios, and purchase timing.

Information You Enter

Financial and vehicle information entered into Car Calculator is used to perform calculations within the app. This information is stored locally on your device so the app can restore your profile and saved options.

Data Collection

Car Calculator does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, or sell personal financial information. The app does not use analytics, advertising, bank connections, or external vehicle APIs.

Data Storage and Deletion

Your profile and saved options remain on your device. You can remove this information using the app's reset action or by deleting the app from your device.

Third-Party Services

Car Calculator does not currently use third-party services to process the information you enter. If third-party services are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe how those services process information.
